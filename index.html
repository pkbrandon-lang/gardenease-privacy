GardenEase

Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

GardenEase is a vegetable-garden app for Southwest gardeners, made by Patrick Brandon (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app collects, why, who helps us process it, how long we keep it, and how to have it deleted.

The short version

  • We store your garden (beds, crops, tasks, notes, and the photos you add) so the app works and syncs.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, and no tracking, and we don’t sell or rent your data.
  • The app doesn’t access your location and doesn’t ask for your name or email.
  • Photos you use for plant identification are sent to Plant.id to get a result.
  • Email us to have everything deleted.

What we collect

An anonymous account

When you first use the app, it creates an anonymous account identified by a random account ID. It isn’t linked to your name, email, or phone number. Your account ID is shown in the app under Settings → Privacy.

Your garden profile

Your USDA hardiness zone, your growing region (such as High desert), the kinds of garden you have (raised beds, in-ground, pots), and your experience level. We use these to tailor planting dates and tips.

What you add to your garden

Your beds, the crops planted in them and their dates, square foot garden plans, tasks (including due dates, repeats, and reminder times), notes, and any custom crops you create.

Photos you add to notes

Photos you attach to a note are stored privately with your account. Before uploading, the app shrinks each photo and saves it as a new image file. This leaves out embedded details the original photo may carry, such as its GPS location and camera information.

Plant identification and health checks

When you use “Identify a plant” or a plant health check, the app shrinks your photo and sends it, through our server, to Plant.id to get a result. We don’t store these photos unless you choose to save the result as a note. We count how many scans your account makes each day so we can apply a daily limit.

Technical information

Like most online services, our hosting provider keeps short-term server logs of requests to the app’s backend, which can include your IP address, the time of the request, and error details. We use them only to keep the service running and fix problems.

While GardenEase is in beta testing through Apple’s TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send with us, as described in Apple’s TestFlight terms.

What stays on your device

  • Zip code and state. If you look up your zone by zip code during setup, the zip code is sent directly from your device to the free zone-lookup service phzmapi.org to find your hardiness zone. Neither the zip code nor the state you enter is saved to your account; they’re only used on your device to suggest a region.
  • Reminders. Task reminders are scheduled as notifications on your device. Nothing is sent to a notification server.
  • Offline copies. The app keeps a copy of your garden and any changes waiting to sync on your device, so it works without a connection.

Permissions

  • Camera: asked only when you choose to take a photo, for a note, plant identification, or a health check.
  • Photos: the app uses the system photo picker, so it only receives the photos you pick.
  • Notifications: asked if you turn on task reminders.
  • The app does not use your location, microphone, or contacts.

Why we use your information

Only to provide the app: saving and syncing your garden, tailoring planting advice to your zone and region, identifying plants and checking their health when you ask, applying the daily scan limit, keeping the service secure, and fixing problems. We don’t use your information for advertising or profiling, and we don’t sell it.

Who processes your information

  • Supabase hosts the app’s backend: your account, garden data, note photos, and the server function that talks to Plant.id. Your data is protected so that each account can only read its own garden. See supabase.com/privacy.
  • Kindwise (Plant.id) receives only the photo you submit for identification or a health check, not your account ID or other data, and returns the result. Kindwise handles these photos under its own terms; see kindwise.com.
  • phzmapi.org receives a zip code if you use the zone lookup, as described above.
  • Apple distributes the app and, during beta testing, provides crash reports and feedback through TestFlight.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your garden data for as long as your account exists, so it’s there when you open the app. When you delete something in the app (a bed, task, note, photo, or custom crop), it’s removed from our database. Daily scan counts are kept with your account. Server logs are kept for a short time by our hosting provider. Deleted data may remain in backups for a limited time before it’s overwritten.

Deleting the app from your phone does not delete your data from our servers. Because your account is anonymous, after deleting the app you won’t be able to get back into that garden.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your account and everything in it, including your photos, at any time:

  • In the app, go to Settings → Privacy and tap Copy next to your account ID.
  • Email GardenEaseSupport@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my GardenEase data” and paste your account ID.

Your account isn’t tied to an email, so we need the account ID to find it. We’ll delete the account and all its data, including photos, within 30 days and confirm by email. If you’ve already deleted the app and don’t have your account ID, email us anyway and we’ll help where we can. An in-app delete option is planned.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Each account can only access its own garden data and photos, photos are stored privately, and the Plant.id key stays on our server. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

GardenEase is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, email us and we’ll delete the account’s data.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll post the new version on this page and update the effective date at the top. For significant changes, we’ll also tell you in the app’s release notes (the “What to Test” notes during TestFlight beta testing) or in the app before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy or this policy: Patrick Brandon, GardenEaseSupport@gmail.com.
